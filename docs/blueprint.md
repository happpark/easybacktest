# **App Name**: AlphaFlow

## Core Features:

- Portfolio Creation & Allocation: Users can search for and select assets (e.g., stocks, gold, crypto), dynamically adjust their allocation weights with percentage input fields and sliders, and view the composition of their proposed portfolio.
- Backtesting Engine & Performance Display: Performs portfolio backtesting simulations based on user-defined inputs. Key performance metrics such as 'Analysis Period', 'CAGR' (Compound Annual Growth Rate), and 'Max Drawdown (MDD)' are calculated and displayed in prominent cards.
- Radar Chart Portfolio Analysis: Visualizes multi-faceted portfolio performance across five key metrics: Attack, Defense, Volatility, Sharpe Ratio, and Dividend Yield, through a large, interactive, semi-transparent, and glowing radar chart (pentagon shape).
- AI-Powered Market Insight Tool: An AI tool that analyzes recent financial news and market data to provide concise, sentiment-driven insights relevant to the assets in a user's selected portfolio, aiding in informed allocation decisions.
- Community Portfolio Discovery: Browse a gallery of various user-submitted portfolios within a grid layout, each featuring a mini radar chart, portfolio title, and accessible search and filter options.
- Detailed Community Portfolio View: Upon selecting a portfolio card from the community gallery, the view expands to display a detailed asset list and further metrics for that specific portfolio.
- Performance Sharing: Enables users to share their backtesting results, portfolio insights, and radar chart visualizations with others through a dedicated share function.

## Style Guidelines:

- Background: A very dark, subtle blue-grey (`#131315`) to create a professional, deep, and 'dark-themed glassmorphism' foundation.
- Primary Color: A robust, professional blue (`#3787E0`) for main interactive elements, text, and primary information, providing clear contrast against the dark background.
- Analogous Accent Color: A bright, vibrant cyan (`#5BE7F2`) for secondary highlights and elements requiring high visual prominence, creating dynamic contrast.
- Additional Accent Colors: Incorporate specific 'electric blue' (`#007BFF`) for glowing effects in charts and key highlights, and 'mint green' (`#7AE9AB`) for positive indicators, growth metrics, and chart segments.
- Headline and Body Font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) to convey a modern, objective, and precise aesthetic, ideal for clear data presentation in a financial dashboard.
- Clean, modern, and geometric outline icons. Consider a frosted or subtly blurred visual style for elements to reinforce the 'glassmorphism' aesthetic where appropriate.
- A professional financial dashboard aesthetic with clear spatial hierarchy. Utilize cards with subtle transparency effects, generous spacing, and distinct sectioning (e.g., 1/3 top for metrics, 2/3 middle for chart) to organize content effectively.
- Smooth transitions for navigation and data updates. Incorporate subtle 'glowing' effects for the Radar Chart and interactive elements, and fluid animations for card expansions in the community feed to enhance the user experience.
